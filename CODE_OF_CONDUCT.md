# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The DMSI community is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for every participant, regardless of identity, background, experience, education, appearance, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate with patience, empathy, and professional respect.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and make room for people with different levels of technical experience.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Respect privacy, attribution, intellectual property, and classroom boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Trolling, insulting language, and bad-faith participation may also be moderated.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any public space where a person is representing the DMSI project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report serious or sensitive conduct concerns privately through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/mralexgarrido/dmsi/security/advisories/new). Include relevant links, dates, and context. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, or other material that violates this code. Enforcement may include a private warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces. Decisions will consider context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and the [Citizen Code of Conduct](https://github.com/stumpsyn/policies/blob/master/citizen_code_of_conduct.md).
